Rembesdalskaka glacier mass balance, 1998-2024
Glacier-wide seasonal and annual balance, in meters of water equivalent (m w.e.).

This file has NO header row. Columns, in order:
  1. year
  2. summer balance (m w.e.)
  3. winter balance (m w.e.)
  4. annual balance (m w.e.)

Note for EES 3350/5350: this file and rembesdalskaka_balance_1963-1999.csv were made by
splitting NVE's single mass balance file (rembesdalskaka_massbalance_nve.csv) into two pieces,
on purpose, to practice combining data that arrives in more than one file.
Data: NVE (Norwegian Water Resources and Energy Directorate), http://glacier.nve.no/viewer/CI/
